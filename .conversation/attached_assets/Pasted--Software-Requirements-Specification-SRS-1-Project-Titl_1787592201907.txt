# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## 1. Project Title

**The Desert Diary by SS — Official Bakery Website**

---

## 2. Project Overview

The Desert Diary by SS is a home-bakery business that accepts customized cake orders, prepares cakes from home and provides customer delivery/pickup according to its actual service arrangements.

The website will act as the bakery's official digital storefront.

The primary objectives are:

1. Present the bakery professionally.
2. Showcase authentic cake work.
3. Allow customers to browse the catalogue.
4. Explain customization options.
5. Make ordering easy.
6. Provide contact and social-media access.
7. Establish a memorable premium visual identity.
8. Provide a responsive experience across desktop and mobile.

---

## 3. Target Users

### Primary Users

Customers looking for:

- Birthday cakes
- Custom cakes
- Theme cakes
- Floral cakes
- Cupcakes
- Bento cakes
- Special occasion cakes

### Secondary Users

People discovering the bakery through:

- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- Printed hoarding
- Promotional posters
- Word of mouth

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### FR-01 — Homepage

The system shall provide a visually engaging homepage containing:

- Brand name
- “by SS”
- Brand tagline
- Hero cake imagery
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Navigation

---

### FR-02 — Navigation

The system shall provide access to:

- Home
- Our Cakes
- Custom Cakes
- Our Story
- Gallery
- Contact

---

### FR-03 — Cake Catalogue

Users shall be able to browse available cake products.

Each product may contain:

- Name
- Image
- Category
- Description
- Flavours
- Sizes
- Price
- Customization status

Only available/verified information shall be displayed.

---

### FR-04 — Category Filtering

Users shall be able to filter cakes by available categories.

Example:

- All
- Birthday
- Custom
- Floral
- Theme
- Cupcakes
- Bento
- Special Occasion

Categories must be based on the actual bakery catalogue.

---

### FR-05 — Cake Details

Users shall be able to view additional information about an individual cake.

---

### FR-06 — Custom Cake Information

The website shall explain the bakery's customization process.

Potential customization parameters:

- Theme
- Colour
- Flavour
- Size
- Message
- Decorative elements

Only supported options shall be displayed.

---

### FR-07 — Gallery

The website shall provide a gallery containing authentic cake photographs.

The gallery shall support:

- Responsive layout
- Image viewing
- Lightbox
- Smooth transitions

---

### FR-08 — Ordering

The website shall provide clear ordering CTAs.

Where applicable, buttons shall open WhatsApp with a prefilled message.

Example:

“Hi! I’d like to enquire about a cake from The Desert Diary.”

The exact WhatsApp number must come from the supplied business information.

---

### FR-09 — Instagram

The website shall link to the bakery's actual Instagram account.

---

### FR-10 — Delivery

The website shall describe delivery only using verified business information.

If delivery details are not available, do not fabricate them.

---

### FR-11 — Responsive Layout

The website shall support:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01 — Performance

The website should load efficiently and avoid unnecessary large assets.

### NFR-02 — Accessibility

The website should support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Alt text
- Accessible controls
- Reduced motion

### NFR-03 — Security

Do not expose sensitive information.

Do not place API keys or secrets in frontend code.

### NFR-04 — Maintainability

Cake data and business data should be separated from UI components.

### NFR-05 — Scalability

The architecture should allow future addition of:

- New cakes
- New categories
- New photographs
- New products
- Customer enquiry forms
- Payment integration
- Online ordering

---

## 6. Visual Requirements

The visual system shall follow the existing bakery branding.

Primary aesthetic:

- Blush pink
- Cream
- Ivory
- White
- Rose/berry pink
- Subtle champagne gold

Design motifs:

- Flowers
- Fine botanical lines
- Hearts
- Soft decorative flourishes
- Elegant frames
- Cake photography

---

## 7. Animation Requirements

Animations shall be:

- Smooth
- Subtle
- Premium
- Responsive
- Performance-conscious

Preferred animation types:

- Scroll reveal
- Parallax
- Image reveal
- Staggered card animation
- Hover interaction
- Modal transition
- Floating decorative elements

Avoid excessive animation.

---

## 8. 3D Requirements

3D should be used selectively.

Possible implementations:

- Layered depth
- Parallax
- Floating decorative elements
- 3D-like cake presentation
- Lightweight WebGL elements

The 3D experience shall never compromise:

- Loading speed
- Mobile usability
- Accessibility
- Content readability

---

## 9. SEO Requirements

Implement:

- Page title
- Meta description
- Open Graph metadata
- Semantic HTML
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Image alt text
- Appropriate structured data where verified business information is available

---

## 10. Content Integrity

The system shall not invent business information.

Never fabricate:

- Prices
- Testimonials
- Reviews
- Address
- Delivery charges
- Delivery radius
- Opening hours
- Flavours
- Ingredients
- Certifications
- Policies

Unknown information should be marked as pending or omitted.

---

## 11. Future Enhancements

Potential future versions may include:

- Online cake customization
- Customer accounts
- Order management
- Payment gateway
- Admin dashboard
- Automated order confirmation
- Customer reviews
- Coupon system
- Inventory management
- Delivery tracking
- Analytics

These are outside the initial MVP unless explicitly requested.

---

## 12. Success Criteria

The website is successful when:

1. A first-time visitor immediately understands what The Desert Diary is.
2. Authentic cake photographs are prominently displayed.
3. Customers can easily browse cakes.
4. Customers can understand customization.
5. Customers can easily contact/order from the baker.
6. The website looks professional on mobile and desktop.
7. Animations feel premium rather than excessive.
8. The website visually matches the existing posters and hoarding.
9. No fabricated business information is presented.
10. The site feels like a genuine premium bakery brand rather than a template.
